PHASE 2 · 13–32 min

2. Calculate the Real Cost — Aaliyah's Nursing Paths

Use these three steps:

Total preparation costAll relevant preparation expenses for the same time period, added together.

Program fees + books/supplies + transportation and other included expenses.

Non-loan fundingMoney available without taking a loan, such as grants, scholarships, savings, or confirmed employer assistance.

Add grants, scholarships, savings, and eligible employer support. Do not include loans.

Funding gapAmount still uncovered after subtracting non-loan funding from total cost.

MAX(0, total preparation cost − non-loan funding).

Worked example: Program cost $9,000 + books $1,000 = $10,000 total. A $3,000 grant plus $2,000 saved = $5,000 non-loan funding. Funding gap = $5,000. This is not automatically the amount to borrow.

Aaliyah is comparing a two-year associate-degree nursing pathway and a four-year university bachelor's-degree pathway. All figures are fictional program totals, not annual figures or real aid awards. Housing and meals are excluded in this classroom comparison.

Federal vs. Private Student Loans

Federal student loans often have borrower protections that private loans do not offer to the same extent. But federal borrowing has eligibility requirements and limits; a student might still face a shortfall. A private loan is not automatically the answer—compare other aid, program cost, and loan terms first.
